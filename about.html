Our story

Craving, Kitchen, Doorstep.
In 30 Minutes.

Stackly was born when our founders waited 90 minutes for a lukewarm pizza and thought, “there has to be a better way.” Today we operate a 5,000-kitchen network with a delivery fleet that treats every box like fine china.

We obsess over temperatures, tipping points, live tracking and allergens — so you never have to. Every meal is a small celebration we’re honoured to deliver.
