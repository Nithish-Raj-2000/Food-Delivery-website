💬 Frequently asked questions

How do I track my order?

Open My Orders from the sidebar � every active order shows a live status (Kitchen, Rider or Delivered). We also toast you the moment your rider is assigned.

How do refunds work?

If an order is cancelled before it's packed, your wallet is credited instantly. For late or wrong deliveries, our support team resolves within 24 hours and auto-credits the amount.

What is Stackly Gold?

Stackly Gold is our rewards tier. Every order earns gold coins that convert into free deliveries, cashback and member-only offers. Track it from your Wallet tab.

How do I add money to my wallet?

Head to Wallet & Rewards, tap Add money and choose an amount. Payments are secured and the balance reflects instantly.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes. Open My Profile from the sidebar, update your address and hit Save Changes. Future orders use the new address automatically.
